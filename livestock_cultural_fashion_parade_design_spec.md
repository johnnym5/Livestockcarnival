# Livestock Cultural Fashion Parade & Master Program Specification

## 1. Page Purpose & Routing
- **Route:** `/fashion-parade` (and featured under `/schedule` & `/attractions`)
- **Objective:** Showcase the intersection of Nigerian cultural textiles, pastoral heritage, animal costume parade floats, and Queen/King of NLC Pageant entries.

---

## 2. Official 3-Day Event Integration

### Day 1: Grand Opening & Heritage Pageantry
- **Ribbon Cutting by Hon. Minister of Livestock Development**
- **Kano Durbar Horsemen Display & Royal Cavalry Pageantry**
- **Parade Floats featuring welfare-conscious animal costume styling**
- **Midway Opening, Live Cultural Acts, Beer Garden & Karaoke Night**

### Day 2: Agro-Investment & Breed Championships
- **Queen and King of National Livestock Carnival (NLC) Pageant**
- **Cattle Formation & Conformation Judging**
- **Sheep and Goat Breed Competitions (Balami, Uda, Yankasa, Maradi)**
- **Dairy & Meat Goat Showmanship Heats**
- **Evening Parade Floats & Agritainment Concert Night 1 (Top Nigerian Artist #1)**

### Day 3: B2B Trade & Grand Finale
- **Commercial B2B Matchmaking & High-Stakes Seedstock Auction**
- **Open Crafted, Visual, Home Arts & Traditional Quilt Judging**
- **Dog & Cat Showmanship Contests**
- **Livestock Judging Grand Championship Awards & Cooperative Grants**
- **Grand Finale Concert (Top Nigerian Artist #2) & Laser Light Show**

---

## 3. Visual Theme & Layout Engine
- **Canvas:** `#FBFBFA` (Soft Alabaster) with `#111827` (Charcoal Obsidian) typography.
- **Hero:** Full-bleed background video/cinematic image of a decorated Kano Durbar horse or Aso-Oke draped bull with gold serif typography.
- **Scroll Physics:** Lenis smooth scrolling with Framer Motion `staggerChildren` to fade in animal showcase cards sequentially.

---

## 4. Component Hierarchy

### A. The Hero Gateway
- **Eyebrow:** RENEWED HOPE CARNIVAL PRESENTS
- **Headline:** Livestock Cultural Fashion Parade
- **Tagline:** "Our Livestock. Our Culture. Our Heritage. One national parade bringing every region's animals, breeds, and traditions onto one stage."
- **Background:** `public/assets/fashion_parade_hero.jpg`

### B. The "One Nigeria" Zonal Motif Grid
- **North:** Camels & Horses (Northern-inspired textiles & handler costumes).
- **West:** Cattle & Horses (Aso-Oke beadwork & Yoruba motifs).
- **East:** Goats & Cultural Displays (Igbo-inspired fabrics & patterns).
- **South:** Specialized Displays (Traditional Niger Delta motifs).

### C. Animal Welfare & Styling Guidelines
- **Rule 1:** *Decorations on the Saddle, Not the Animal.*
- **Rule 2:** *Comfort Meets Culture.* (Handlers in Ankara matching their goat's loose fabric collar).
- **Rule 3:** *Themed Enclosures for Fowl & Small Ruminants.*
